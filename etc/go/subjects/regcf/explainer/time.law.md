Every dollar figure above is really a figure _as at a date_. Four dated
amendments move the numbers, and each carries its own Federal Register
authority:

- **[2016-05-16](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L143 "verbatim")** —
  commencement, by
  [80 FR 71388 (Rel. 33-9974), Part 227 text at 71537, instr. 4](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L114 "verbatim").
  Before this date there is no Regulation Crowdfunding to ask about.
- **[2017-04-12](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L118-L120 "verbatim")** —
  an inflation adjustment, by
  [82 FR 17545 (Rel. 33-10332), instrs. 5-6](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L118 "verbatim").
- **[2021-03-15](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L122-L124 "verbatim")** —
  the substantive amendment, by
  [86 FR 3496 (Rel. 33-10884), instr. 3](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L122 "verbatim").
  The offering ceiling rose to
  [$5,000,000](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L151); the investment limit
  changed shape from _lesser_ of income and net worth to _greater_ of; and
  accredited investors were carved out of the cap entirely.
- **[2022-09-20](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L126-L128 "verbatim")** —
  a further inflation adjustment, by
  [87 FR 57394 (Rel. 33-11098), instrs. 2-3](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L126 "verbatim").
  It moved six figures, not five: the income/net-worth cut point, the minimum
  permitted investment, the per-investor cap, and all three
  financial-statement ceilings — the last three of which are printed as dated
  tables in **Pictures**. It deliberately left the offering ceiling alone, on
  the Commission's view that the increase the previous year had already
  [more than account for inflation](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L60-L62 "verbatim").

**And one change that moves no number and still changes the answer.** The
[COVID-19 temporary rules (Rule 201(z)/(bb), 2020-05-04 through 2022-08-28) are deliberately NOT modelled](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L64-L65 "verbatim")
here, so for a rule date inside that window
and an offering inside the affected band the corpus refuses part of the
disclosure question rather than guessing. That is described under **What must be
disclosed**. It belongs on this list too: "four amendments" counts the ones that
move a figure, and this is a fifth and a sixth instrument that change what the
encoding is willing to say.

**Why this is not a footnote.** An inflation adjustment sounds like a rounding
exercise. Consider an investor with income of
[$110,000](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L1242) and net worth of
[$115,000](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L1242). Before the last
adjustment both figures cleared the cut point, so the second limb applied and
their cap was [$11,500](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L1250). After it,
income sits _below_ the raised cut point, so the first limb applies and their
cap is [$5,750](src:jl4/examples/legal/regcf/regcf.l4#L1251).

Raising a threshold halved that person's capacity. Both answers are correct for
their date, and no "last updated" stamp on a web page can express that.
