A regulation is not the whole of the law that applies to it. Courts construe it,
the agency's staff publish interpretive guidance, no-action letters and
compliance-and-disclosure interpretations settle questions the text leaves open,
and proposed amendments sit in flight for months. An encoding that captures only
the text of the rule captures a real thing, and not the whole thing.
