# Source terms — Supplemental Hospitalization Cash Policy (synthetic)

**Unlike every other row in this corpus, the terms here are DETERMINED rather than undetermined.**
The Singapore statute rows (`sg/pdpa-2012`, `sg/penal-code-1871`) record `UNDETERMINED` because the
Crown-copyright question has not been settled. This row does not have that problem, because its
source is not a statute — it is a synthetic instrument printed in two academic papers, and both
carry explicit licences.

There are **two** source documents and they carry **different** terms. That distinction is the whole
content of this file.

## 1. The original — CC BY 4.0, reproduction permitted with attribution

> Oliver R. Goodenough & Preston J. Carlson, *"Words or code first? Is the legacy document or a code
> statement the better starting point for complexity-reducing legal automation?"* (2024).
> PMC10894687 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10894687/

Appendix A of that paper prints **"ILLUSTRATION 1: SUPPLEMENTAL HOSPITALIZATON CASH POLICY"** — the
policy this row encodes. The article states it is distributed under the **Creative Commons
Attribution License (CC BY 4.0)**, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ — verified against
the PMC full text on 2026-08-31.

CC BY 4.0 permits reproduction and adaptation, including commercially, **provided attribution is
given**. Inert style quotes the policy inline, so this encoding reproduces the text rather than
paraphrasing it; the attribution above is therefore required, not courteous, and it is repeated in
the repository `NOTICE`.

## 2. The variant actually encoded — arXiv non-exclusive licence

> Manuj Kant, Sareh Nabi, Manav Kant, Roland Scharrer, Megan Ma & Marzieh Nabi, *"Towards Robust
> Legal Reasoning: Harnessing Logical LLMs in Law"*, arXiv:2502.17638 (2025).
> Appendix A.1, presented there as "Simplified Chubb Hospital Cash Benefit Policy".

**The text we encoded is this modified variant, not the original.** The arXiv listing carries
`arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive license 1.0`
(https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/) — checked 2026-08-31. That licence grants
arXiv the right to distribute; it is **not** a Creative Commons licence and grants third parties no
redistribution right of its own.

That paper prints the policy **without citing Goodenough & Carlson for it.**

## 3. What we conclude, and what we do not

**What we rely on.** The operative text of this policy originates in a CC BY 4.0 work. Our right to
reproduce it, including in modified form, comes from that licence, and the attribution in §1
discharges its condition. We do not rely on the arXiv paper for any permission.

**What is NOT settled here, and should not be read as settled.** Whether the two modifications
recorded in `NOTES.md` (the excision of the benefits section; the age threshold moved from 75 to 80)
attract any separate right of their own in the modifying authors is a question this file does not
answer. It is probably academic — a deletion and a changed numeral are thin material — but "probably
academic" is not a legal opinion and nobody here has given one. If this row is ever used for anything
beyond research, get that looked at.

**No claim is made about the encodings themselves.** The `.l4` files are our own work and carry the
repository's licence. The quoted policy text inside them carries the terms above.
