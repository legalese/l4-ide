The page banner says "official version". The site's Disclaimer page (`https://www.jerseylaw.je/pages/Disclaimer.aspx`, read 2026-09-23) says, verbatim:

> The contents of this website are made available for information purposes only. Nothing within this site should be relied upon as constituting verified or official text.

Neither statement affects the licence. Together they are a reason to read this row as an encoding of the text as published, and not as the law itself.
