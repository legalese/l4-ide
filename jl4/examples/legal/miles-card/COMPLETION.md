**Basis.** MECHANICAL, OVER NINE DOCUMENTS, IN BUNDLE ORDER. Each PDF of the source bundle has a `pdftotext -layout` rendering beside it as <stem>.txt. Every unit below was produced by running a stateful outline parser over those .txt files: it matches a clause marker at the start of a line — `N.`, `(r)`, `a.` or `a)`, or a dotted `N.N.` — keeps a stack of open markers, and closes a level when a token succeeds an open sibling (so a `(ii)` after a `(i)` replaces it at the same depth, and a `19.` after an `18.` returns to the top level whatever the page indentation does). Page furniture ('Updated on 7 August 2026', '## RESTRICTED ##', 'PUBLIC', 'Page 3 of 6') is skipped, and an alphabetic token that is neither a single letter nor a Roman numeral is rejected, which is what stops a wrapped line beginning 'date. The transaction date…' from reading as a marker. A unit's title is the first twelve words of the marker's own line plus as many continuation lines as it takes to reach twelve, quoted and not paraphrased. THE UNIT ID IS <document-stem>:<clause as the source numbers it>, because this subject's source is nine documents rather than one and the schema carries a single source.document_id; that field names the lead document (the subject's own source_url) and the prefix carries the rest. Units run in bundle order across documents and source order within each. FOUR DEPARTURES FROM THE PARSER'S OUTPUT, all additive and all listed here so the count can be re-derived. (1) Unnumbered material the parser cannot see was added by hand: dbs-yuu-card-tnc's Appendix 1 and its two closing paragraphs; uob-ladys-cards-tnc's Schedule 1; posb-passion-yuu-tnc's Appendix 1 and its clauses 7(b)(i) and 7(b)(ii), whose markers carry no full stop and so match nothing; citi-rewards-10x-tnc's travel-related footnote to clause 6; and six units of citi-rewards-exclusion-list — the closing paragraph of clause 1 and the five footnotes to clauses 2 and 3, which carry operative matching rules. > **The enumeration itself is incomplete.** TWO THINGS THIS ENUMERATION DOES NOT REACH, and a reader should not take this unit count as coverage of them. Enumerating at row level was rejected because it would put several hundred MCC codes into the roadmap and bury the clause structure; the cost is recorded here instead of being hidden. SECOND, SOME PROGRAMMES HAVE NO SOURCE AT ALL. Of the eight the subject sidecar names, UOB PRVI Miles and Citi PremierMiles have no document here, and neither does Standard Chartered Smart, which spec §4.3 discusses and the sidecar does not list. None was fetched (source bundle, note 3), so there is nothing to enumerate for them and their absence is invisible in the coverage strip below. PAssion POSB is a different case: it is enumerated in full from a document whose own Promotion Period closed on 30 September 2025 — see external-modification entry E-lapsed-posb-promotion. NO UNIT HERE NAMES A MODULE, AND THAT IS NOT BECAUSE THERE ARE NONE. As at 2026-09-21 11:24 SGT, nine .l4 files were present in jl4/examples/legal/miles-card/ and were being written in the same worktree at the same minute as this deposit. Not one is named by a unit below, because no unit-to-module mapping has been checked and a mapping asserted without checking is exactly the unfalsifiable coverage claim this artifact exists to replace. Two consequences follow. Every operative unit reads `deferred` even where a module may already cover it, so the coverage strip UNDERSTATES what has been encoded rather than overstating it — the safe direction. And every unit that ought to read `inert` reads `out-of-scope` instead, because the schema refuses `inert` without a module path; each such unit carries a note saying so, and flipping them is a one-line edit once the module that carries the inert text is known. ONE NESTING IS UNCERTAIN RATHER THAN MISSING: dbs-yuu-card-tnc:9(f)(i) and 9(f)(ii) are indented in the extraction as limbs of 9(f), which is the MCC limb, but their content is a transaction-description match list and a residual discretion, neither of which is MCC-based. They may be further limbs of clause 9 itself. The ids follow the extraction's indentation; whoever encodes clause 9 should read the PDF's layout before citing them.
