    16. The mechanics of the awarding and calculation of DBS Points is illustrated in the table below.

          Base Rewards – All Spend                1X DBS Point                    Next working day

          Bonus Rewards – Overseas Spend          Additional 2X DBS Points        Next working day

    17. Base Rewards – All Spend:
           a) Base 1X DBS Point will be awarded for every S$5 of retail spend, awarded on the next working day
               when transaction is posted to the Card.
           b) Base DBS Points are calculated based on the total transaction amount divided by S$5 and
               multiplied by 1 DBS Point, rounded down to the nearest whole number.

    18. Bonus Rewards – Overseas Spend:
           a) Bonus 2X DBS Points will be awarded for every S$5 equivalent in overseas spend, awarded on the
               next working day when transaction is posted to the Card.
           b) Bonus DBS Points are calculated based on the total transaction amount divided by S$5 and
               multiplied by 2 DBS Point, rounded down to the nearest whole number.

    19. Bonus Rewards – Online Spend:
           a) Bonus 9X DBS Points (for local online spend) or 7X DBS Points (for overseas online spend) will be
               awarded for every S$5 of online spend, credited by the end of the following calendar month.
           b) Online spend will be accumulated for the month and awarded bonus points in the following calendar
               month, capped at the first S$1,000 of online spend per calendar month.
           c) Online spend must be charged to your card within the calendar month, based on transaction date
               and successfully posted to the Card Account at the time of points computation.
           d) Bonus DBS Points are calculated based on the total transaction amount after accumulation for the
               calendar month divided by S$5 and multiplied by 7 DBS Points for overseas spend or 9 DBS Points
               for local spend, rounded down to the nearest whole number.
