  [P-NOJOIN] lossy — Split_1
      No converging parallel gateway was drawn for this RAND because one of its branches reaches the join by 2 different routes — an interrupting boundary event, a ROR, or a lapse timer — of which at most one will fire, whereas a parallel join would wait for every one of them. A join that waits for a token nothing will send is a deadlock, which is worse than an undrawn conjunction.
      lost: the conjunction as a drawn gateway. No branch here can breach, so every token is still consumed before the instance ends and the conjunction does hold — it is simply not drawn.
